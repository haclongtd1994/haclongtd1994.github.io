Twist Truyện

Publishing tool of the TikTok channel "Twist Truyện · Drama Hoạt Hình" (short animated drama stories in Southern Vietnamese), for the creator's own account only.

Twist Truyện is a small home-studio tool made by Hung Phan. It publishes the short animated story series (motion comics) that the creator produces with AI tools to the creator's own TikTok account, in episode order, with captions, hashtags and the AI-generated content label. It is not offered to other users.

What it does with TikTok, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • signs in to the creator's account (with the creator's consent) and reads the basic profile and the posting options TikTok allows for that account;
  • uploads a finished video and its caption, marked as AI-generated content, with the privacy level the creator chose;
  • checks the publishing status of that upload.
