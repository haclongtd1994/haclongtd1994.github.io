ToneCast - Support

ToneCast has two modes on your iPhone or iPad. Edit photos works on photos from any camera or phone: looks, light and colour, crop, and on-device AI retouch (auto beauty, even skin tone, spot healing, erasing people or objects). Control a Nikon Z connects to Nikon Z-series cameras over Wi-Fi or a USB cable: live view, exposure settings, shooting, writing Picture Control presets into the camera's Custom slots, and developing each new photo on your device. All editing runs on the device; no photo is uploaded.

Contact: henry.embedded.dev@gmail.com - we usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone/iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, camera model and firmware version, and what you were doing when the problem happened (••• › Log › Copy all, then paste it in your e-mail, helps a lot).

Getting started

Editing photos: on the first screen tap Edit photos and pick photos from your library (no permission needed). Choose a look or tap Auto, then fine-tune in Adjust, Crop, Skin and Erase. Saving to Photos asks once for permission to add photos.

Connecting a camera:

  1. On the camera: Network menu > Connect to smart device > turn on Wi-Fi.
  2. On the iPhone/iPad: Settings > Wi-Fi > join the camera's network.
  3. In the app: pick the camera from the list (or type its IP address, usually 192.168.1.1) and tap Connect. Confirm the pairing on the camera the first time.

No camera at hand? Tap "Try Demo mode" on the first screen.

FAQ

The app can't find my camera.
Check Settings > ToneCast > Local Network is on, and that the iPhone is joined to the camera's Wi-Fi (not your home Wi-Fi). Then type the IP address manually.

Which cameras are supported?
Nikon Z cameras: Z9, Z8, Z7II, Z7, Z6III, Z6II, Z6, Zf, Zfc, Z5II, Z5IIc, Z5, Z50II, Z50, Z30 and ZR, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. Tested on: Nikon Z5II (firmware 1.20). Some features differ by model (for example the newest Picture Controls exist only on recent bodies); please tell us how it goes, with the log from ••• (More) › Log.

How do I connect with a USB cable?
On the camera set Network menu › USB to MTP/PTP (not "iPhone", which is only for Nikon's own app), then plug the camera into the iPhone or iPad with a USB-C cable. The app connects by itself and asks once for permission to control the camera. While it is connected, the camera's dials and buttons may be locked; they come back when you disconnect or unplug.

What does "Develop new photos" do?
Turn it on in ••• (More) and pick the look under "Preset for new photos". Each photo you take - with the app or the camera's own shutter - is downloaded (RAW when the camera records it), developed on the device with that preset plus deep colour / skin smoothing as you chose, and shown for review. Untouched, it is saved to Photos after a few seconds (with the RAW attached); touch the screen to choose another look first, or Discard.

It says the RAW file can't be decoded.
Some iOS versions only read the Z5II's Lossless compressed NEFs. Tap "Switch the camera to lossless RAW" in the review, or set Photo shooting menu > NEF (RAW) recording > Lossless compressed. Meanwhile the camera's JPEG is used.

Developed photos aren't saved.
Allow Settings > ToneCast > Photos > "Add Photos Only".

Colours on the phone differ from the camera.
Try ••• › Develop new photos › Calibrate camera colours (camera on a tripod; it takes one RAW + JPEG per Picture Control and puts your settings back afterwards).

Is writing a preset safe?
Writing replaces the chosen Custom slot. Use Backup first to save your current slots to the Files app.

Where are my backups, imported presets and photos?
Files app > On My iPhone (or iPad) > ToneCast.

How do I remove ads?
Subscribe to Pro (US$3.99 a month or US$29 a year in the US; the app shows the price in your currency). New subscribers get 7 days free, once per Apple ID; cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don't want to continue. Pro removes all ads, unlocks the AI assistant (composition tips and preset suggestions over live view) and Colour by AI (photos imported from the camera's card are colour-graded on the device), and shares before/after cards without the "Edited with ToneCast" line. Everything else - the whole photo editor and AI styles included - is free (AI styles show an ad when turned on).

I bought Pro but still see ads / changed iPhone.
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel Pro?
iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > ToneCast > Cancel Subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com
